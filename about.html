[ 01 // THE ORIGIN & INSPIRATION ]

Rooted in Campus Heritage. Engineered for the Open Web.

Lincoln is a Computer Science student at the University of the West Indies, Mona, balancing algorithm design, late-night code reviews, and the realities of student life with an obsession for resilient software systems. Rather than treating computer science purely as an academic exercise, he set out to build fast, tactile digital assets that solve real operational headaches.

The name and brand identity draw direct inspiration from the iconic brown pelican of the UWI crest—a historic regional symbol of quiet resilience and nurturing vigilance. Pelican Script takes that campus heritage and re-engineers it through a modern, cyber-terminal lens: vigilant against page bloat, unyielding on sub-second speed, and dedicated to elevating local enterprises.

Across Jamaica and the wider Caribbean, high-performing businesses frequently find themselves held back by slow, fragile website templates that stall out on spotty mobile data. Pelican Script was built to bridge that gap with hand-crafted code, transparent fixed pricing, and 100% digital asset ownership.
